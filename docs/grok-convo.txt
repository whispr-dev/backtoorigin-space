Paste a URL, domain, IP, prefix, ASN, or mailbox and it tells you what that is, then follows it.

Redirects show every hop: status, time, and headers, through to the final page. http://github.com goes 301 to https://github.com.
DNS covers address, alias, mail, and name-server records, plus the chain from name to address.
Routing shows the prefix, the network that announces it, the registry, and the links out to RIPEstat. An address or ASN anywhere in the results can be traced again with one click.
A bare number is read as an ASN or a decimal IP, and the other reading is one click away. Private and local addresses are explained on the page and are not sent out.

Browsers cannot send ICMP or raw TCP/UDP, so this is not a traceroute. Hops come from a public redirect checker, DNS from Cloudflare’s resolver, and routing from RIPEstat.
The page is by whispr.dev, with the SkeleTrace panel linking to skeletrace.com. Try http://github.com, 1.1.1.1, or AS13335.
